What we do

Four programs, each with a clear purpose, audience, objectives, and intended outcomes.

Beyond the LabelPublication and storytelling

Our publication amplifies lived experiences and invites willing participants to share their stories. Written content educates the public and challenges stereotypes, and every contributor can define their own experience.

Design for DignityCommunity and inclusion

We build inclusion with the community. Students, teachers, staff, administrators, families, and organizations help shape the solution.

Our cycle: listen, ask, design, get feedback, improve.

Workshops & EventsEducation and engagement

Interactive online sessions, plus in-person events in schools, libraries, hospitals, community spaces, and partner locations.

ChaptersCommunity expansion

A chapter extends our mission into a local community. Each has a clear purpose, engages its community, follows shared systems, reports its progress, and stays accountable.

How we work

Every initiative moves through the same seven steps.

  1. IdeaIdentify a need or opportunity.
  2. Program designDefine the purpose, audience, and intended outcomes.
  3. DevelopmentResearch, write, design, and prepare.
  4. OutreachCommunicate and engage the intended audience.
  5. ImplementationDeliver the program or initiative.
  6. FeedbackListen to participants and the community.
  7. ImprovementUse what we learned to make the next version better.
