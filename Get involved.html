    Our Teams/Departments

    The Unlabel Network constitutes of various functioning departments, which handles the TUN's activities.

    The Creative TeamThe Creative Team translates approved ideas and information into visual communication.
    Editorial TeamThe Editorial Team handles TUN's written and informational substance.
    Programs & Initiatives TeamTurning ideas into actual programs.
    Social Media & Communications TeamResponsible for: •Social media strategy •Communications planning •Publishing and scheduling •Platform adaptation •Promoting events and programs •Audience engagement •Monitoring responses •Public communication

    • Participate actively in your team’s work
    • Represent TUN responsibly
